ProbeVision privacy policy

Hands-free multimeter reading for PCB checksheets

Last updated: 29 October 2026

ProbeVision helps a tester fill in a PCB sanity checksheet by reading a multimeter's display with the phone camera. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera. The camera watches the multimeter display and the board's QR code. Frames are processed on the phone to read the digits and the serial number, then discarded. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Microphone. If you use voice mode, the app listens for a few command words ("next", "back") using your phone's built-in speech recognition, which the app asks to run offline. ProbeVision does not record or store audio. Your phone's speech service (usually Google's) handles recognition under its own terms.
  • Your checksheets. Procedures, batches and readings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They leave the phone only when you choose to export a checksheet and pick where to send it.
  • Settings stay on your phone.

ProbeVision has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics of its own, and it does not sell or share your data.

Google libraries

The app uses Google ML Kit (text recognition and barcode scanning), which runs on the phone. Like many Google SDKs, ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic and performance information (for example, the API used, how long it took, and the device model) to maintain and improve the service. It does not send your camera images, readings or checksheets. See Google's ML Kit terms.
